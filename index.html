About Brittany

A genuine conversation, not a clinical script

Therapy, to me, feels like a genuine conversation between two people. I show up as myself — curious, engaged, and thoughtful. I'll ask questions, offer new perspectives, and help you build insight and practical tools so you can feel more like yourself again.

I've spent the last 10 years working alongside clients navigating anxiety, big life transitions, and the particular weight of trying to hold everything together. Whether you're preparing for a baby, recovering from burnout, or simply tired of putting everyone else first, my goal is to help you understand the patterns that keep you stuck — and build a life that feels more like your own.

My work centers on three things: mindfulness & grounding, boundaries & communication, and self-compassion. Session to session, we'll come back to these as the foundation for lasting change.

I'm based in Bergen County and work with clients throughout New Jersey and New York, including Bergen County and New York City, all through secure telehealth.

My Approach

Types of Therapy

I draw from several evidence-based approaches and tailor them to what you need, session to session.

CBT

Shifts the thought-feeling-behavior patterns that keep anxiety in place.

DBT

Skills for tolerating distress and navigating relationships when things feel intense.

ACT

Makes room for hard feelings while moving toward what matters most to you.

Compassion-Focused

Builds self-compassion as a real skill for a loud inner critic.

Person-Centered

A warm, non-judgmental space that follows your lead.

Motivational Interviewing

Builds your own motivation for change, at your own pace.

Strength-Based

Builds on what's already working for you.
